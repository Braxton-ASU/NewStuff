Once upon a time, there was a town, and in that town there was a Biergarten, and in that Biergarten there was a mouse.
Exciting his Porche with hid Hund a man took a seat in the Biergarten, the Hund saw the mouse and started barking. 
The mouse panicked, dropped his broetchen, and having no better ideas, ran into the Porsche and locked the doors!
Eventually the Hund grew hundemuede, while the mouse sped away in the Porsche with Rammstein blasting through the speakers to satisfy an incessant Ohrwurm.
The mouse drove down the Hauptstrasse so fast that he crashed into a Baeckerei, sending Sauerkraut and Apfelkuchen flying everywhere!
"How could you!" shouted the Mehlhase, as she surveyed the mess!
